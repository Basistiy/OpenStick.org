## How to Add Your Video to this Library

We welcome video creators! To add your video:
1. Open this page in GitHub by clicking **"Edit this page"** below.
2. Add a new section with your video's title, a brief 2-sentence summary of what viewers will learn, and the `<VideoEmbed platform="youtube|bilibili" id="..." />` tag.
3. Submit your pull request!
