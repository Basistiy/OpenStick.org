## Why OpenStick & MSM8916?

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Cortex-A53 Quad-Core Power" icon="rocket">
    Powered by the **Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 (MSM8916)** with 4x ARM Cortex-A53 64-bit cores @ 1.2 GHz, 512 MB – 1 GB LPDDR3 RAM, and 4 GB – 8 GB onboard high-speed eMMC storage.
  </Card>

  <Card title="The $7 Pi Zero 2 W Alternative" icon="star">
    When electronics shortages hit makers during the **2025–2026 AI boom**, standard single-board computers were scalped or backordered. MSM8916 wingles became the premier accessible Linux SBC.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integrated 4G LTE & Wi-Fi" icon="signal">
    Unlike bare SBCs that require expensive cellular HATs, OpenStick dongles feature an onboard 4G LTE Cat 4 baseband modem, 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth 4.0.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mainline Linux & OpenWrt" icon="laptop">
    Run full Debian GNU/Linux, postmarketOS (mainline kernel), or lightweight OpenWrt to create pocket travel routers, VPN endpoints, SMS gateways, and local servers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Self-Contained Thumb Drive" icon="seti:powershell">
    No fragile microSD cards, no bare circuit boards, and no extra cables required. Plug straight into any 5V USB-A power supply or computer port.
  </Card>

  <Card title="100% Community Driven" icon="pencil">
    Every single article on this website has an **"Edit this page"** button. Add your own hardware teardowns, test points, or custom builds directly via GitHub!
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
