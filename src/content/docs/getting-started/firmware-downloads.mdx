### What the 1-Click Installer Does Automatically:
- **Double-Click Native Launchers**: Includes `run.bat` (Windows), `run.command` (macOS), and `run.sh` (Linux).
- **Zero-Solder EDL Reboot**: Transitions your dongle into Qualcomm `9008` Emergency Download Mode via ADB, Fastboot, or SSH.
- **Full 1:1 eMMC Backup**: Dumps your complete raw eMMC (`full_emmc.bin`) locally before modifying a single byte.
- **Automatic IMEI & Radio Calibration Injection**: Extracts your stick's unique factory cellular partitions (`modemst1`, `modemst2`, `fsg`, `fsc`, `persist`, `sec`) and injects them directly into the clean OpenStick Debian image so 4G LTE works immediately on first boot.
